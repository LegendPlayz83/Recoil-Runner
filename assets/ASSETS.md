# Assets – place files here yourself (do not redistribute!)

## assets/sprites/   (png/webp/jpg)
background  Background image (e.g. No Signal Horror Background)
rock        Rock tile -> crop one tile from the tileset OR set the region in scripts/game_art.gd (ROCK_REGION)
spike       Spike sprite, tip pointing upward (optional)
player      Sprite sheet (Pixel Plains: 24x24 frames, region in game_art.gd: PLAYER_REGION)

## assets/music.mp3
Background music (looping, menu + gameplay). Simply place it in `assets/music.mp3` – adjust volume in the pause menu.

## assets/sounds/   (ogg/wav/mp3)
shot, land, death, checkpoint, goal, empty, reload
